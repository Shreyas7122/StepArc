03 — Shape language

The concave cut.

Every brand card is a rounded square with one corner curved inward — like a tile pulled from a larger pattern. It's the brand's signature, used consistently on social, marketing, and in-app.

Rules
  • One concave cut per card. Never two, never on opposite corners.
  • Cut radius = 25–50% of the card's shortest side.
  • The cut reveals the surface behind the card — never a third color.
  • Use the cut to frame imagery, a stat chip, or a "what's behind".
